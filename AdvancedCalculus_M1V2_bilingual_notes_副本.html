Part I
Important Ideas & Useful Facts
核心概念与重要事实

i Smooth Functions 光滑函数 / 可导函数

A function \(y=f(x)\) is smooth光滑 at \(x=a\) if the derivative导数 \(f'(a)\) exists, so the curve has a tangent line切线 at \(\big(a,f(a)\big)\). The tangent then closely approximates the curve nearby. We then say \(f\) is differentiable可微 / 可导 at \(x=a\).

Some authors require all higher derivatives to exist for smoothness; we use only this weaker condition. 部分教材要求所有高阶导数都存在才算"光滑"，本课程不作此要求。

ii Continuous Functions 连续函数

A function is continuous连续 at \(x=a\) if both \(f(a)\) and \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to a}f(x)\) exist and are equal:

\[ \lim_{x\to a} f(x) = f(a) \]

Informally: the curve can be drawn without lifting the pen off the page. 直观地说，曲线可以"一笔画成、笔不离纸"。

A curve can be continuous without being smooth. E.g. \(f(x)=|x|\) is continuous everywhere but not smooth at \(x=0\) — a sharp corner尖角 where the derivative fails to exist. 连续不一定光滑：绝对值函数在原点有尖角，不可导。

iii The Extreme Value Theorem 极值定理 / 最值存在定理 (EVT)

If \(acontinuous on the closed interval \([a,b]\), then \(f\) achieves a maximum最大值 \(f(c)\) and a minimum最小值 \(f(d)\) for some \(c,d\in[a,b]\).

Intuitive via the "no pen-lifting" picture. A rigorous proof needs formal limit definitions, covered in analysis数学分析. 闭区间上的连续函数，最大值和最小值一定存在。

iv Derivative Vanishes at Extrema 极值点处导数为零 (费马引理)

An extreme value极值 is any max or min, global or local. Theorem: if \(f(c)\) is an extreme value for some interior point \(c\in(a,b)\) and \(f'(c)\) exists, then

\[ f'(c)=0 \]

Geometrically the tangent line is horizontal ("in balance") at the extremum. 极值点处切线水平。

⚠️ 注意这个前提：「如果 f′(c) 存在」

这条定理只在导数存在时才成立。如果某点不可导（比如尖角），极值可能藏在那里却不满足 f′=0。这正是后面例题 2 必须计算 \(f(0)\) 的根本原因。

v Closed Interval Method 闭区间法 / 最值求法

To find the absolute max/min全局最值 of a continuous \(f\) on \([a,b]\), it suffices to compare \(f(c)\) over these candidate points候选点:

Candidate 候选点Condition 条件
\(c=a,\; c=b\)the two endpoints端点
interior \(c\)\(f'(c)=0\)  →  stationary point驻点
interior \(c\)\(f'(c)\) does not exist  →  non-differentiable point不可导点

The highlighted third row is the one students most often forget. 高亮的第三类点最容易被遗漏！

vi Rolle's Theorem 罗尔定理

If \(f\) is continuous on \([a,b]\), differentiable on \((a,b)\), and the endpoints agree \(f(a)=f(b)\), then

\[ f'(c)=0 \quad\text{for some } c\in(a,b) \]

If the function returns to the same height, somewhere in between the tangent must be horizontal. Follows from the EVT plus fact (iv). 两端点等高 ⟹ 中间必有一点切线水平。

vii The Mean Value Theorem 中值定理 (MVT)

If \(f\) is continuous on \([a,b]\) and differentiable on \((a,b)\), then for some \(c\in(a,b)\):

\[ f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a} \]

The right side is the average rate of change平均变化率; the MVT says this is hit instantaneously as the derivative somewhere inside. 平均变化率必在某一瞬间被瞬时变化率（导数）达到。

🚗 Traffic analogy: a car covering 111 km in one hour must, at some instant, have been going 111 km/h. Rolle's Theorem is the special case \(f(a)=f(b)\). 测速原理；罗尔定理是其特例。

viii Consequences of the MVT 中值定理的推论

For differentiable \(f\) on \((a,b)\):

If…then… 结论
\(f'(x)>0\) everywhere\(f\) is increasing单调递增
\(f'(x)<0\) everywhere\(f\) is decreasing单调递减
\(f'(x)=0\) everywhere\(f\) is constant常值函数
\(f'(x)=g'(x)\) everywhere\(f(x)=g(x)+C\)  (差一个常数)积分常数的来源

(a)(b) underpin sign diagrams符号表 / 增减表 in curve sketching; (d) explains the "+C" in integration. 前两条支撑画图的增减表，最后一条是积分中"+C"的由来。

EX 1Proof: derivative vanishes at an extremum证明：极值点导数为零
Setup 设定

Assume \(f(c)\) is a maximum at interior \(c\), and \(f'(c)\) exists. Pick small \(h>0\) with \(c\pm h\in[a,b]\). Form two secants割线 \(S_1\) (left) and \(S_2\) (right).

Squeeze 夹逼

As \(h\to 0\), both secant slopes tend to \(f'(c)\). Since \(f(c)\) is a max:

\[ \text{slope of }S_1\ge 0 \;\Rightarrow\; f'(c)\ge 0, \qquad \text{slope of }S_2\le 0 \;\Rightarrow\; f'(c)\le 0 \]

Thus \(0\le f'(c)\le 0\), forcing \(f'(c)=0\). \(\blacksquare\)  用左右割线斜率把导数夹在 0 与 0 之间。

EX 2 ★Optimise \(f(x)=x^3-|x|\) on \([-1,2]\)本题就是 f(0) 之谜
The function 分段表达
\[ f(x)=x^3-|x|=\begin{cases}x^3-x & x>0\\[2pt] x^3+x & x\le 0\end{cases} \]
Derivative 求导
\[ f'(x)=\begin{cases}3x^2-1 & x>0\\[2pt] 3x^2+1 & x<0\\[2pt] \textbf{undefined} & x=0\end{cases} \]

Stationary point驻点: \(3x^2-1=0\) with \(x>0\) gives \(x=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\). And \(3x^2+1>0\) always, so no root on the left.

★ 为什么 f(0) 绝不能省略？

因为 \(f(x)\) 含 \(|x|\)，在 \(x=0\) 处是尖角，导数不存在。根据闭区间法第三类候选点（不可导点），\(x=0\) 必须检查。极值"藏"在不可导点时，并不满足 \(f'=0\)，光靠求驻点会漏掉它！

对比：之前 \(x^{-n}\) 在 \(0\) 处无定义（函数不存在）→ 算 \(f(0)\) 无意义；这里 \(f(0)=0\) 有定义但不可导 → 必须算。

Curve is continuous but not smooth at \(x=0\). Here \(f(0)=0\) is a local max — not the global answer, but you only know that after checking. 在 \(x=0\) 处连续但不光滑；\(f(0)=0\) 是局部极大，算过才知道它不是全局最值。

EX 7Rolle ⇒ MVT (the conjugation trick)用罗尔定理证明中值定理

Define the tilted function

\[ h(x)=f(x)-(x-a)\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a} \]

Then \(h(a)=h(b)=f(a)\), so Rolle gives \(h'(c)=0\) for some \(c\). Since \(h'(c)=f'(c)-\dfrac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}\), the MVT follows. \(\blacksquare\)

Idea: tilt the curve so its endpoints are level, apply the easy case (Rolle), then untilt. This is the Conjugation Principle共轭原理 — transform a hard problem into an easy one, solve, then transform back. 把曲线"压平"成罗尔定理的特例，解完再变回去。
