Part I
Core Rules & Useful Facts
核心法则与重要事实

i Definition of the Derivative 导数的定义

The derivative导数 of \(f\) at \(x\) is the limit of the average rate of change as the interval shrinks to zero:

\[ f'(x)=\lim_{h\to 0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h} \]

Geometrically it is the slope of the tangent line切线斜率 at the point \(\big(x,f(x)\big)\). 导数就是切线的斜率。

All the rules below are shortcuts that save us from computing this limit every time. 下面所有法则都是为了省去每次都算极限的麻烦。

ii The Power Rule 幂法则

For any real exponent \(n\):

\[ \frac{d}{dx}\,x^{n}=n\,x^{n-1} \]

Bring the power down in front, then subtract one from the power. 把指数拿到前面当系数，再把指数减一。

Works for negative and fractional powers too: \(\frac{d}{dx}\sqrt{x}=\frac{d}{dx}x^{1/2}=\frac{1}{2}x^{-1/2}\). 负指数、分数指数同样适用。

iii Constant & Sum Rules 常数法则与和法则

Constants pull out, and derivatives split across sums:

\[ (c\,f)'=c\,f', \qquad (f+g)'=f'+g' \]

The derivative of a constant alone is zero, since a flat line has no slope. 常数的导数为零——水平线没有斜率。

iv The Product Rule 乘积法则

The derivative of a product is not the product of derivatives. Instead:

\[ (uv)' = u'v + uv' \]

"Derivative of the first times the second, plus the first times derivative of the second." 前导后不导，加前不导后导。

⚠️ 最常见的错误

很多人会写成 \((uv)'=u'v'\)，这是错的！乘积法则必须有两项相加。

v The Quotient Rule 商法则

For a quotient \(\dfrac{u}{v}\):

\[ \left(\frac{u}{v}\right)' = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^{2}} \]

"Low d-high minus high d-low, over low squared." 低乘高导，减高乘低导，除以低的平方。

Order matters! The numerator is \(u'v-uv'\), not \(uv'-u'v\) — swapping them flips the sign. 顺序不能反，否则差一个负号。

vi The Chain Rule 链式法则

For a composite function复合函数 \(f\big(g(x)\big)\):

\[ \frac{d}{dx}f\big(g(x)\big) = f'\big(g(x)\big)\cdot g'(x) \]

Differentiate the outer function, keep the inside unchanged, then multiply by the derivative of the inside. 先导外层、内层不变，再乘以内层的导数。

🧅 Think of peeling an onion: work from the outside in, multiplying as you go. 像剥洋葱，由外向内逐层相乘。

Part II
Worked Examples
例题精解
EX 1Power rule on a polynomial幂法则求多项式

Differentiate \(f(x)=3x^4-5x^2+7x-9\).

Apply term by term 逐项求导
\[ f'(x)=12x^3-10x+7 \]

The constant \(-9\) vanishes. 常数项 \(-9\) 求导后消失。

EX 2Product rule乘积法则

Differentiate \(f(x)=x^2\sin x\). Let \(u=x^2,\;v=\sin x\), so \(u'=2x,\;v'=\cos x\).

\[ f'(x)=2x\sin x + x^2\cos x \]
EX 3 ★Quotient rule商法则

Differentiate \(f(x)=\dfrac{x^3}{x^2+1}\). Here \(u=x^3,\;v=x^2+1\), so \(u'=3x^2,\;v'=2x\).

Plug into the formula 代入公式
\[ f'(x)=\frac{3x^2(x^2+1)-x^3(2x)}{(x^2+1)^2}=\frac{x^4+3x^2}{(x^2+1)^2} \]

This is exactly the practice problem from your study session. 这正是你之前练习的那道题。

EX 4Chain rule链式法则

Differentiate \(f(x)=(3x^2+1)^5\). Outer \(=(\cdot)^5\), inner \(=3x^2+1\).

\[ f'(x)=5(3x^2+1)^4\cdot 6x = 30x(3x^2+1)^4 \]

Outer derivative \(5(\cdot)^4\), times inner derivative \(6x\). 外层导数乘内层导数。

EX 5Inverse-function trick: \(\tan^{-1}y\)反函数求导

Given \(y=\tan x\), find \(\dfrac{dx}{dy}\). Recall the inverse rule \(\dfrac{dx}{dy}=\dfrac{1}{dy/dx}\).

\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=\sec^2 x \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{dx}{dy}=\frac{1}{\sec^2 x}=\cos^2 x \]

This matches the handwritten example you studied. 与你手写笔记中的例子一致。
