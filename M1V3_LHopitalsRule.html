Part I
Important Ideas & Useful Facts
重要思想与有用事实

i L'Hôpital's Rule — the \(\tfrac{0}{0}\) form 零比零型

Suppose \(\displaystyle\lim f(x)=\lim g(x)=0\), and \(\displaystyle\lim \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}\) exists (finite, \(\infty\), or \(-\infty\)). Then:

\[ \lim \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \lim \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)} \]

The limit may be two-sided (\(x\to a\)), one-sided (\(x\to a^+\) or \(x\to a^-\)), or at infinity (\(x\to\pm\infty\)). 极限可以是双侧、单侧或趋于无穷。

The power of the rule: a complicated fraction's difficulty often dissolves — completely or in stages — by differentiating top and bottom. 妙处在于：对分子分母分别求导后，复杂的极限往往会层层化简。

ii L'Hôpital's Rule — the \(\tfrac{\infty}{\infty}\) form 无穷比无穷型

Suppose instead \(\displaystyle\lim f(x)=\lim g(x)=\infty\), and \(\displaystyle\lim \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}\) exists. Then the same conclusion holds:

\[ \lim \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \lim \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)} \]

This form is especially useful for comparing rates of growth. 这一形式在比较增长速率时特别有用。

iii Running times of algorithms 算法运行时间

An algorithm's running time \(T=T(N)\) estimates the number of steps for input size \(N\), and typically \(\displaystyle\lim_{N\to\infty}T(N)=\infty\). 运行时间 \(T(N)\) 表示输入规模为 \(N\) 时的步骤数，通常随 \(N\) 增大而趋于无穷。

To compare two programs with running times \(f(N)\) and \(g(N)\), we study the asymptotic limit:

\[ \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{f(N)}{g(N)} \]

treating \(N\) as a real variable and applying calculus — L'Hôpital's Rule is ideal here. 把 \(N\) 当作实变量处理，用微积分（尤其洛必达法则）来分析。

iv Interpreting the comparison 如何解读比较结果

With \(\displaystyle\lim_{N\to\infty}f(N)=\lim_{N\to\infty}g(N)=\infty\):

极限结果含义 Meaning
\(\lim \frac{f}{g}=0\)\(f\) 增长更慢 → \(f\) 的算法更快/更高效
\(\lim \frac{f}{g}=\infty\)\(f\) 增长更快 → \(f\) 的算法更慢/更低效
\(\lim \frac{f}{g}=K>0\)两者增长速度相当（效率可比）

Note: \(\lim \frac{f}{g}=0\) if and only if \(\lim \frac{g}{f}=\infty\). 注意这两个说法等价。Sometimes the limit does not exist, and one falls back on bounds or approximations. 有时极限不存在，则需借助上下界或近似。

Part II
Worked Examples
例题精解
EX 1A 0/0 limit, with a check零比零型并验证

Since \(\lim_{x\to2}(x^2-4)=\lim_{x\to2}(x-2)=0\), apply the \(\tfrac{0}{0}\) form:

\[ \lim_{x\to2}\frac{x^2-4}{x-2}=\lim_{x\to2}\frac{2x}{1}=\frac{2(2)}{1}=4 \]
Check by factoring 用因式分解验证
\[ \lim_{x\to2}\frac{(x+2)(x-2)}{x-2}=\lim_{x\to2}(x+2)=4 \]
EX 2Applying the rule twice两次应用法则

Both numerator and denominator vanish at \(x=-1\), so apply \(\tfrac{0}{0}\) — and the result still vanishes, so apply again:

\[ \lim_{x\to-1}\frac{x^3-3x-2}{x^3+x^2-x-1}=\lim_{x\to-1}\frac{3x^2-3}{3x^2+2x-1}=\lim_{x\to-1}\frac{6x}{6x+2}=\frac{-6}{-4}=\frac{3}{2} \]
EX 3 ★The famous limit sin x / x著名极限

Since \(\lim_{x\to0}\sin x=\lim_{x\to0}x=0\):

\[ \lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin x}{x}=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\cos x}{1}=\cos 0 = 1 \]

This recovers the foundational limit of trigonometric calculus, normally hard to prove directly via the squeeze theorem. 这正是三角函数微积分的基础极限，直接用夹逼定理证明相当困难。

EX 4One-sided limits to ±∞单侧极限趋于正负无穷
\[ \lim_{x\to1^+}\frac{\ln x}{(x-1)^2}=\lim_{x\to1^+}\frac{1/x}{2(x-1)}=\lim_{x\to1^+}\frac{1}{2x(x-1)}=+\infty \]

From below, \(x-1<0\), so the same calculation gives \(-\infty\). 从左侧逼近时 \(x-1<0\)，结果为 \(-\infty\)。

EX 5 ⚠Must check the form first!必须先检查形式

不检查就求导 = 出错

应用 \(\tfrac{0}{0}\) 形式前，必须确认分子分母都趋于零。盲目反复求导会得到错误答案。

After one valid step, the form is no longer \(\tfrac{0}{0}\) — so evaluate directly:

\[ \lim_{x\to1}\frac{x^3-2x+1}{x^2+x-2}=\lim_{x\to1}\frac{3x^2-2}{2x+1}=\frac{3-2}{2+1}=\frac{1}{3} \]

The wrong answer \(3\) comes from differentiating a second time when the form was no longer indeterminate. 错误答案 3 源于在已非未定式时又多求了一次导。

EX 6Exponential beats polynomial指数增长压倒多项式
\[ \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{e^x}{x^2+3x+4}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{e^x}{2x+3}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{e^x}{2}=\infty \]

In general \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{e^x}{p(x)}=\infty\) for any polynomial \(p\) with positive leading term: "exponential growth outstrips polynomial growth." 指数增长压倒任何多项式增长。

EX 7Logarithm grows slower than linear对数比线性慢
\[ \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\ln x}{x}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{1/x}{1}=0 \]

"Logarithmic growth is slower than linear growth." 对数增长比线性增长慢。

EX 8When L'Hôpital fails洛必达失效的情形

For \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\sqrt{1+x^2}}{x}\), L'Hôpital just flips numerator and denominator back and forth — no progress. 反复应用只会把分子分母翻来翻去，毫无进展。

Instead, manipulate directly 改用直接变形
\[ \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\sqrt{1+x^2}}{x}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\sqrt{\frac{1}{x^2}+1}=\sqrt{0+1}=1 \]

Moral: L'Hôpital is powerful but not a cure-all — sometimes another technique is needed. 洛必达虽强，但并非万能，有时需另辟蹊径。

EX 9Algorithm comparison: 2^N vs N²算法比较

Compare \(f(N)=2^N\) and \(g(N)=N^2+5N+3\):

\[ \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{N^2+5N+3}{2^N}=\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2N+5}{(\ln 2)2^N}=\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2}{(\ln 2)^2\,2^N}=0 \]

So \(f(N)=2^N\) grows faster — the polynomial-time algorithm is far more efficient. 指数时间增长更快，多项式时间的算法效率高得多。

Part III
The Growth Hierarchy
增长速率排序（由慢到快）
1\((\ln N)^k\)对数及其幂 · 最慢
2\(\sqrt{N},\; N^p\;(0根号 / 分数次幂
3\(N,\; aN+b\)线性
4\(N^2,\; p(N)\)多项式
5\(2^N,\; e^N\)指数 · 最快

每一层都被它下面一层压倒：指数 ≫ 多项式 ≫ 线性 ≫ 根号 ≫ 对数。
This ordering is what L'Hôpital's Rule lets us prove rigorously. 这一排序正是洛必达法则能严格证明的。
